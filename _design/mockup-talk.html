Shipping fast without breaking things

How small teams can deploy dozens of times a day with feature flags, good tests and a culture that treats incidents as lessons.

1.0What this talk covers

Most teams slow down as they grow because every deploy feels risky. This talk walks through the habits that let my last team go from weekly releases to 30+ deploys a day without more incidents.

  • Trunk-based development with short-lived branches
  • Feature flags as a release tool, not a config system
  • A test pyramid you can actually afford to run on every commit
  • Blameless incident reviews that change behaviour
"The deploy button should be the most boring button in the company."
